## 1. Verdict

The deck of 15 October was a 5.0 on the ETH scale with a clear route to 6 (grader report). The deck of 16 October is more complete at the edges and weaker in the middle. The edges gained: the CASP14 chart is back on slide 2, every pasted figure names its source, the summary carries the CC BY line, the title carries the repository, the references are on two closing slides, the exam and audience slides are laid out, the handover sits after the triangles. The middle lost: the three architecture slides that the council called the best in the deck (IPA with its formula, FAPE with its formula, the Evoformer block) were replaced by Supplementary Information block diagrams that nobody past the third row can read, the key lines of almost every slide were rewritten in a register the deck had deliberately avoided, and about fifteen statements were introduced that the sources do not support or contradict. The backups shrank from eleven to five, and two of the five now contain wrong formulas. As it stands the deck would not pass a careful fact check, and it reads as generated text in the places where a reader judges the authors.

The spine is intact: four facts, four design choices, their ablation cost, AlphaFold 3's verdict, summary. That is the asset. Everything below is about restoring the finish around it.
