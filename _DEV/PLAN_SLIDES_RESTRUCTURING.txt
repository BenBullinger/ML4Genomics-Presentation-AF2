4. SLIDE 12: FRAME ALIGNED POINT ERROR (FAPE) GROUNDED IN THE TRAINING LOOP
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
TARGET SLIDE: Slide 12 ("Frame Aligned Point Error (FAPE)")
SPEAKER: Ben Bullinger (Part II)

A. RESTRUCTURING THE VISUAL NARRATIVE
- Instead of showing FAPE as an isolated abstract clamping formula, present it as
  THE END-TO-END SUPERVISION LOOP of the Structure Module:
  1. The Structure Module outputs predicted frames {T_k} and atoms {x_i}.
  2. Ground truth structures provide true frames {T_k^true} and atoms {x_i^true}.
  3. Rather than running a global Kabsch alignment, FAPE places a "camera" on
     every single residue frame k, evaluating atom i in local coordinates:
     x_i^(k) = T_k^{-1} o x_i.

B. THREE PHYSICAL CONSTRAINTS EXPLAINED INTUITIVELY
1. Why NOT Global RMSD? (Numerical & Physical Failure of Kabsch SVD)
   - Global Kabsch alignment requires SVD during backpropagation. If two
     singular values coalesce, gradients diverge.
   - Global alignment allows an error in a floppy, unconstrained terminal tail
     to rotate the entire protein, corrupting gradients across the stable core.
   - FAPE localizes errors: an error in domain B does not penalize domain A.
2. The 10 Angstrom Clamping Horizon
   - Errors are capped at d_clamp = 10 Angstroms.
   - Once two domains are further than 10 A apart, the gradient is exactly zero.
     The network does not waste capacity trying to resolve large rigid body
     displacements before fine-grained local packing is solved.
3. Enforcing Stereochemical Chirality (No Mirror Images)
   - Distance matrices are reflection-invariant (O(3) symmetry), producing 50%
     unphysical D-amino acid mirror images.
   - Frames are constructed via right-handed cross product: e_3 = e_1 x e_2.
   - Under a mirror reflection, atomic coordinates invert, but the right-handed
     basis does NOT invert. The local z-coordinate flips sign: (x_i^(k))_z -> -(x_i^(k))_z,
     triggering a massive FAPE penalty that eliminates mirror structures.

5. ROLE OF MATHEMATICS & BACKUP SLIDES (THE FORMAL DEFENSE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
In the main deck (Slides 8, 11, 12), the slides focus on:
- Layer schematics (Supp Fig 2, Supp Fig 8),
- Information flow (inputs -> operations -> outputs),
- Physical constraint intuition (rotations, chirality, loop closures).

The formal mathematical machinery is fully preserved and highlighted in the
BACKUP SLIDES, ready for instant invocation during Q&A:

- BACKUP 1 (IPA Invariance Proof):
  Algebraic proof of exact SE(3) cancellation:
  ||(Tg o Ti) o q - (Tg o Tj) o k||^2 = ||Tg o (Ti o q - Tj o k)||^2 = ||Ti o q - Tj o k||^2
  and the inverse pullback: (Tg o Ti)^{-1} o Tg o Tj = Ti^{-1} o Tg^{-1} o Tg o Tj = Ti^{-1} o Tj.
- BACKUP 2 (Chirality & FAPE Metric Lower Bound):
  Gram-Schmidt frame reflection breakdown, non-negativity on quotient space
  R^{3M} / SE(3), and the formal lower bound: L_FAPE >= min_{R,t} RMSD(R X + t, Y).
- BACKUP 3 (Triangle Updates & 2D Metric Consistency):
  Hadamard path marginalization sum_k z_ik (x) z_jk enforcing metric closure
  d_ik <= d_ij + d_jk prior to coordinate generation.
- BACKUP 4 (Non-linear Ablations & Recycling):
  Detailed breakdown of the -16.3 GDT catastrophic collapse when removing both
  IPA and recycling simultaneously (coupling between iterative trunk depth and 3D frames).
- BACKUP 5 (AlphaFold 3: Diffusion vs Inductive Bias):
  Why AF3 dropped frames/IPA/FAPE for 3D all-atom diffusion (chemical generality
  across DNA/RNA/ligands) and the cost (4.4% chirality violations, clash filters).
